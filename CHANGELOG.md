## 0.0.1

* Initial release with placement-based ads APIs.
* Added Google Mobile Ads support for banner, native, interstitial, rewarded,
  rewarded interstitial, and app open ads.
* Added initial AppLovin MAX support for interstitial, rewarded, and app open
  ads.
* Added UMP consent, optional iOS ATT request, fullscreen cooldowns, clear APIs,
  and example app.
